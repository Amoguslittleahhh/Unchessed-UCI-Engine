Blackjack Rules
1. Core (Basic) Rules
Objective
  • Beat the dealer's hand without exceeding 21
  • If your hand exceeds 21, you "bust" and lose automatically
  • If the dealer busts, all remaining players win
Card Values
  • Number cards (2-10): Face value
  • Face cards (J, Q, K): Worth 10 points each
  • Aces: Worth 1 or 11 points (whichever benefits your hand more)
Gameplay Flow
  • Place your bet before cards are dealt
  • Each player and the dealer receives two cards
  • Players act first, deciding to Hit, Stand, Double Down, or Split
  • Dealer reveals their hand and must follow house rules (typically hitting until 17+)
  • Compare hands to determine winners
Standard Dealer Rules
  • Dealer must hit until they reach 17 or higher
  • Dealer must stand on 17 or higher
  • In some casinos, dealer hits on "soft 17" (Ace + 6)
Payouts
  • Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card): Usually pays 3:2
  • Winning hand (higher than dealer): Pays 1:1
  • Push (tie with dealer): Bet is returned
  • Loss: Bet is forfeited
Basic Player Options
  • Hit: Take another card
  • Stand: Keep current hand and end turn
  • Double Down: Double bet and take exactly one more card
  • Split: If first two cards match, split into two hands
  • Surrender: Forfeit half bet and fold hand (if allowed)
  • Insurance: Side bet against dealer blackjack (if dealer shows Ace)
2. Intermediate Rules
Splitting & Resplitting Rules
  • Split pairs to create two separate hands
  • Must place additional bet equal to original wager
  • Can split up to 3 times (making 4 hands total)
  • Aces can usually only receive one card per split hand
  • Resplitting Aces may or may not be allowed
Doubling Rules
  • Double on any two-card total (9, 10, 11, 12-20)
  • Restricted doubling (only on 9, 10, 11)
  • Double after split (DAS) may or may not be allowed
  • Double for less than full amount (usually not allowed)
Surrender Rules
  • Early Surrender: Surrender before dealer checks for blackjack
  • Late Surrender: Surrender after dealer checks for blackjack
  • Usually forfeit half of original bet
Dealer "Peek" & Hole-Card Rules
  • Classic: Dealer checks hole card immediately if showing 10 or Ace
  • European: Dealer receives hole card but doesn't check until after players act
Deck Count & Penetration
  • Games played with 1-8 decks shuffled together
  • More decks slightly favor the house
  • Penetration refers to how much of deck is dealt before reshuffling
3. Advanced / Hard Variants
European Blackjack
No dealer hole card until after players make decisions
Atlantic City
8-deck game with late surrender and resplitting allowed
Vegas Strip
4-deck game with liberal doubling and splitting rules
Vegas Downtown
Single-deck game with favorable player rules
Single-Deck Classic
One deck, often with favorable rules for skilled players
Spanish 21
All 10s removed from deck, 21 always wins, bonus payouts
Blackjack Switch
Play two hands simultaneously and switch second cards
Pontoon
British variant with different terminology and rules
Super Fun 21
Player-favorable rules including redoubling and bonus payouts
Double Exposure
Both dealer cards are face-up, but with modified rules favoring house
4. Quick Checklist
Player-Friendly Features to Look For
Blackjack pays 3:2 (not 6:5)
Dealer stands on all 17s
Late surrender available
Double after split allowed
Resplit aces allowed
Single-deck or double-deck games
House-Favorable Rules to Avoid
Blackjack pays 6:5
Dealer hits soft 17
No surrender option
6+ decks in shoe
Restrictive doubling rules
5. Glossary of Key Terms
Hard Hand: Any hand without an Ace, or with an Ace counted as 1 point
Soft Hand: A hand containing an Ace counted as 11 points
Natural/Blackjack: An Ace plus any 10-value card as first two cards
Push: A tie between player and dealer hands
Bust: Exceeding 21 points in a hand
Hole Card: Dealer's face-down card
Shoe: Device holding multiple decks of cards
Penetration: Percentage of deck dealt before reshuffling
House Edge: Statistical advantage the casino holds over players
