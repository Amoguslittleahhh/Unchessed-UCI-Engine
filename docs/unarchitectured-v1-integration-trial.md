# Unarchitectured v1 root-hint integration trial

## Promotion status

This is a **default-unreachable engineering trial**, not a production feature.
The normal UCI path still calls `search::go` with no neural hints and does not
load the Unarchitectured checkpoint. There is no UCI option enabling this path.
No strength, Elo, clock-safety, or SPRT claim is made.

The actual deployment CPU is not known or reachable from this environment.
All latency and integrated-search figures below are from the sandbox's
2-logical-CPU Intel Xeon host and must not be projected onto another machine.

## Implemented trial infrastructure

- `search::go_with_root_hints` accepts a policy-only first-pass ordering signal.
  Every legal move remains in the root list. From the second iterative-deepening
  pass onward, completed alpha-beta scores are the only ordering signal.
- Synchronous callers must provide `preprocessing_elapsed`; it is included in
  the same soft/hard deadline and UCI info time as search.
- `UnarchitecturedHintWorker` owns a persistent model instance and uses a
  one-request bounded `try_send` queue. Submission never waits for queue space.
- Background results require an exact key match over position hash, legal
  actions, rating, time class, policy persona, and elastic exit. A stale result
  cannot be consumed for a different position.
- Nothing constructs the worker or hint-search entry point in normal UCI play.

## Fixture-disjoint calibration trial

The harness uses eight legal FENs spanning openings, middlegames, tactics,
endgames, and promotion-adjacent positions. Neither frozen Python parity fixture
is included. Labels are generated by this engine's HCE search at depth 4 over
all legal root moves.

This is only a smoke calibration. The local checkout has no training-membership
manifest and no Stockfish binary, so the corpus cannot be claimed training-
provenance-disjoint and the HCE labels are not production teacher labels.

| Exit | Policy top-1 | Policy top-3 | Mean teacher-best rank | Regret MAE | WDL Brier |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2/128 | 0.5000 | 0.5000 | 4.3750 | 0.530366 | 0.714232 |
| 4/192 | 0.5000 | 0.6250 | 4.3750 | 0.545688 | 0.742925 |
| 8/256 | 0.6250 | 0.7500 | 6.0000 | 0.675764 | 0.724530 |

Eight positions are far too few to set a deployment threshold. These figures
prove the harness executes and expose why representative, provenance-disjoint
calibration remains a real promotion blocker.

## Precharged integrated search trial

The controlled harness compares ordinary HCE search with a shallow 2/128 policy
hint under `movetime 75`. Neural input conversion and inference begin before
search and their complete elapsed time is charged to the search deadline.
Runs alternate baseline/hinted order across the eight positions.

Three sandbox runs:

| Run | Baseline mean depth | Hinted mean depth | Baseline NPS | Hinted NPS | Inference/position | Best-move agreement |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 9.000 | 9.875 | 1,043,625 | 1,849,800 | 3.243 ms | 7/8 |
| 2 | 8.750 | 9.750 | 769,410 | 1,453,673 | 3.854 ms | 7/8 |
| 3 | 9.000 | 9.750 | 1,040,276 | 1,433,117 | 3.205 ms | 7/8 |

This tiny trial did not reproduce the old catastrophic depth collapse, but the
7/8 best-move agreement also demonstrates that time-limited ordering can change
search results. It therefore does **not** justify enabling the hint. NPS is
search-tree-dependent and is reported only as instrumentation, not strength.

## Tactical checks

New non-ignored tests verify that:

- an adversarial policy ordering cannot suppress a forced mate in one;
- a precharged hint preserves the only legal move and returns within a bounded
  wall time; and
- asynchronous submission is nonblocking and exact-key lookup rejects stale
  results.

This is not a full mate/only-move suite, so `runtime_safety_suite` remains false.

## Unavailable gates

- Deployment-CPU benchmarking: target hardware was not supplied or reachable.
- Production calibration: no provenance-disjoint labeled corpus is available.
- Paired-game SPRT: neither `cutechess-cli` nor `fastchess` is installed, and
  the trial intentionally has no UCI enablement to test.

The next promotion step belongs on owner-supplied deployment hardware and data,
followed by a default-off UCI candidate, integrated depth/NPS and tactical suite,
and only then an isolated paired-game SPRT.

```bash
cargo test -p unchessed-core --release \
  benchmark_fixture_disjoint_exit_calibration -- --ignored --nocapture

UNCHESSED_INFERENCE_THREADS=2 cargo test -p unchessed-core --release \
  benchmark_precharged_shallow_root_hint_search -- --ignored --nocapture
```
